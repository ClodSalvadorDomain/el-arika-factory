# Showroom scroll brief

Self-authored, not interviewed. Built from the request: "a glass texture like iPhone, simple, luxurious and elegant" and "a scroll down option for a different feel".

- **Vibe:** pearl glass, factory warmth, red as the only accent.
- **Grammar:** distinct scenes (not one continuous flight). The shop pages stay a dense, price-first megastore; the showroom is the "different feel" you scroll into from the hero, the nav and the homepage act.
- **Assets:** no footage, so the scrubbed "film" is a live 3D scene instead of video.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Device | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Arrival | flow + parallax planes | curiosity | liquid-metal emblem floating between a moving gradient and a glass card |
| 2 Build (peak) | pin, longest span (3.6 viewports) | delight | the sofa assembles under the reader's own scroll, then takes the fabric they pick |
| 3 Origin | flow + wipe reveal | trust | plain prose about factory-direct prices, a quiet act before the range |
| 4 Rooms | pan | abundance | lateral travel through every room with "from" prices |
| 5 Close | pin + pointer, drift to night | resolve | the page goes dark and holds on one action |

**Peak:** "You scroll and the sofa literally builds itself, then you pick the colour."

**Signature move:** scroll-driven assembly of a procedural 3D sofa with a live fabric dock and a chapter rail.
